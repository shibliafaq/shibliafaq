## Measurements already done — do NOT re-derive these

These cost a lot of iterations. They are in `compose_map.py` with comments.

| confusion | wrong signal | measured separator |
|---|---|---|
| roof vs autumn tree | both dark warm brown | **saturation**: buildings are slate/stone/plaster S<0.34, foliage is saturated |
| autumn foliage vs dirt road | both saturated orange | **hue**: foliage H=17-20, dirt/sand H=28-40, split at 26 |
| tree canopy vs grass | both green | **texture**: local std of V over 9x9, grass 0.00-0.04, canopy 0.13+, split at 0.085 |

Two architectural points that matter:
- Classify **per pixel**, then vote per tile. Per-tile colour averaging destroys
  thin features — it made the road vanish entirely.
- **Exclude object pixels from the terrain vote.** Terrain is what is UNDER the
  buildings and trees. A tile with a tree on grass votes grass.

Also settled: the Cute Fantasy terrain sheets are 3 cols x 6 rows of 16px; rows
0-2 are the 3x3 nine-slice and the pure centre fill is **(1,1)**. Edge tiles
bake in shoreline and grass keylines, so never use them as fill.

## What still needs fixing, roughly in priority order

1. **No autumn palette.** The kit is summer/spring; the reference's orange top
   third can't be matched. Either hue-shift a subset of tree sprites at load
   time, or decide the composition is summer and drop the autumn look. This is
   the most visible gap.
2. **No shore band.** Water is nine-sliced but meets land abruptly.
   `map-kit/01-terrain-tiles/beach.png` (5x3, water->sand) is loaded but unused.
3. **Farm rows are flat** `FarmLand_Tile` with no crop props scattered on top.
4. **Buildings picked by width alone**, so a tall narrow blob can get a squat
   wide house. Should score on width AND height AND aspect.
5. **Props repeat visibly** — one flat list, not weighted by terrain type.

Also worth considering: whether tracking the Gemini map this literally is even
the right goal, or whether it should only guide the region layout and road while
the detail is composed freely from the kit.

## Constraints carried over

- Scale is anchored on a **door = 2.0m -> 12px/m -> adult 20px**. Never derive
  scale from the hero sprite; that is circular and cost a whole session.
- Sprite clearance must test a sprite's **drawn box**, not its anchor cell, or
  trees swallow buildings.
- No unclothed `Body_A` character assets. Don't repeat the same villager
  everywhere.

## When the map is finally good

`composed_map.png` is NOT wired into the site yet — deliberately. Swapping it in
is one line in `v2/src/modules/pixel/walkmap.js`. But **`road_path.json`,
`stops.json` and `npcs.json` all store coordinates in Gemini-map pixels and must
be re-traced** against the new map. `v2/lab/trace.html` already has four modes
for this: Road, Stops, People, Animals.
